# SASSA Help Hub Content & News Guidelines (2026)

To maintain AdSense compliance and provide high-value content to our users, follow these weekly update procedures.

## 1. Weekly News Update Process
Every Monday and Thursday, perform a search for "Latest SASSA News" or "SASSA Announcements" to identify trending topics.

### Recommended Search Queries:
- `latest SASSA news South Africa [Month] 2026`
- `SASSA SRD R370 grant updates today`
- `SASSA payment dates confirmed [Month] 2026`
- `Department of Social Development SONA 2026 highlights`

### Priority Topics for 2026:
- **Integrity Rollout:** Updates on biometric verification and automated cross-checks.
- **Grant Thresholds:** Any changes to the R624/R620 income limits.
- **Payment Disruptions:** Warnings about ATM downtimes or system maintenance.
- **Scam Alerts:** New phishing tactics targeting SASSA Gold Cards.

## 2. Content Standards
- **Uniqueness:** Never copy-paste from other sites. Rewrite news in a helpful, guide-like tone.
- **Structure:** Use H1 for titles, H2 for subheaders, and bullet points for lists.
- **Actionability:** Every news article should tell the user *what to do* (e.g., "Check your status", "Update bank details").
- **Redirection:** Link to internal tools like the [Status Checker](/) or [Payment Calendar](/payment-calendar) to improve user engagement.

## 3. AdSense Compliance (Anti-Thin Content)
- **Word Count:** Aim for at least 300 words per major news update.
- **E-E-A-T:** Cite official sources (e.g., "According to the SASSA Parliamentary briefing...") to establish authority.
- **Non-Deceptive:** Ensure ads are clearly labeled "ADVERTISEMENT" and placed at least 50px away from interactive "Check Status" buttons.

## 4. Maintenance
- Regularly update `public/data/updates.json` with new entries.
- Ensure the `lastmod` date in `public/sitemap.xml` is updated whenever new articles are added.
- Keep `paymentDates.json` current for the upcoming two months.
